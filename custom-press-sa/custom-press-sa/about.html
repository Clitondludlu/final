About us

A proudly registered South African business, dedicated to delivering high-quality printing and apparel solutions.

Our story

Built for people building their own brand

We specialise in sublimation printing, vinyl transfers and direct-to-film (DTF) printing — producing vibrant, long-lasting results for any project. Whether you're launching your own clothing line, creating branded gear, or expanding your printing capabilities, we offer the products, machinery and expertise to help you succeed.

What we offer

  • Custom prints for clothing, merchandise and promotional items
  • Blank apparel for businesses, brands and resellers
  • Printing equipment such as heat presses and vinyl cutters, including durable combo machines
  • Design services — from logos to full websites
